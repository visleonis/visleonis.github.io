Featured product

TIVRE-Basket Pro

TIVRE-Basket Pro is a tool designed by and for strength and conditioning coaches, basketball coaches, and sports scientists. It standardizes, controls, and monitors the scientifically validated TIVRE-Basket basketball-specific endurance test in real time, directly on the court.

Unlike treadmill-based laboratory tests or generic field tests, the TIVRE-Basket protocol reproduces the specific metabolic demands of competitive basketball, including changes of direction and intermittent bouts of maximal effort alternating with recovery periods.

Main features

Audio-guided pacing: progressive sound signals help players maintain the required running pace throughout each stage of the test.

Synchronized group assessment: evaluate up to 12–14 players simultaneously on the court, with a total test duration of less than 30 minutes.

Real-time monitoring: follow the reference player's position on the circuit and view the elapsed test time, distance covered, current stage, and completed stations.

Adaptability and training mode: adjust the starting speed to different performance levels or repeat stages at a constant intensity to train basketball-specific endurance.

Scientific evidence

The TIVRE-Basket protocol has demonstrated high test-retest reliability in elite male basketball players, with coefficients of variation below 5%. It provides objective reference data for training planning, monitoring, and assessing fitness throughout the season.

Vaquera, A., Villa, J. G., Morante, J. C., Thomas, G., Renfree, A. J., & Peters, D. M. (2016). Validity and test-retest reliability of the TIVRE-Basket test for the determination of aerobic power in elite male basketball players. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, 30(2), 584–587. https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000001078

Privacy

TIVRE-Basket Pro Privacy Policy

Product: TIVRE-Basket Pro

Publisher: Vis Leonis

Last updated: 2026-11-27

TIVRE-Basket Pro is designed to perform its core functions entirely offline and does not require a user account. It does not send personal data or test results to Vis Leonis or automatically transmit them to external services. It does not use advertising, analytics, telemetry, or user tracking.

As an integral part of its functionality, TIVRE-Basket Pro allows users to create and manage athlete profiles and groups, conduct tests, and retain a history of test results. The application stores athlete names and surnames, group information, optional heart rate monitor identification numbers, test dates, exercise times, and distances covered locally on the device. This information is used to organise athletes, record test results, and review performance over time. The application does not automatically collect heart rate measurements.

Users may choose to export test results as a CSV file to a location of their choice. Exported files are under the user's control and can be deleted by the user. The application does not automatically upload or send these files to Vis Leonis or any external service.

Local application data is retained on the device until deleted by the user. Users can delete athlete profiles, groups and stored test results through the relevant application features, or clear all local application data through Android settings. Previously exported CSV files must be deleted separately. Android cloud backup is disabled in the application. Any device-to-device transfer is subject to the Android version and device manufacturer's implementation. Vis Leonis does not receive or have access to locally stored data or device transfers.

The application contains optional external links to Google Play and this privacy policy. Opening these links is subject to the privacy practices of the relevant external services. If users contact Vis Leonis by email, including to request access to beta testing, their email address and any information they voluntarily provide are used to respond to their request or manage their testing access.

For privacy-related questions, please contact visleonisdev@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
